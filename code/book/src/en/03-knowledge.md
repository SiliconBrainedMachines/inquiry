# Knowledge — Is Knowing Remembering or Computing?

RAG, MemGPT, vector databases — we're building memory for machines. But what is it to "know" something? This chapter convokes Plato, Locke, Hume, Kant, and Popper to examine what knowledge is, where it comes from, and why asking a machine to refute rather than confirm is the strongest tradition in epistemology.

The emergent tool: **falsification thinking.** Counteract the LLM's confirmation bias by invoking Popper's asymmetry between verification and falsification.
