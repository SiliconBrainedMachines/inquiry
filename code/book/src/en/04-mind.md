# Mind — Is Anyone in There?

The alignment debate, consciousness claims, "AGI." Does the machine think? This chapter convokes Descartes, Searle, Nagel, Chalmers, and Turing to examine what thinking is, what consciousness requires, and why recognizing the machine's limits is neither dismissal nor anthropomorphism.

The emergent tool: **intellectual humility.** Recognizing what the machine does and doesn't do — the precise midpoint between worship and contempt.
