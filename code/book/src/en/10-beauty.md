# Beauty — Can the Unfelt Be Created?

AI-generated art, music, prose. Is it creative? Is it art? This chapter convokes Plato, Aristotle, Hegel, Adorno, and the wabi-sabi tradition to examine what beauty is, what creation requires, and why taste — knowing what to keep and what to discard — remains irreducibly human.

The emergent tool: **creative judgment.** The capacity to evaluate, select, and refine AI output. Not generation — curation. Not creation — direction.
