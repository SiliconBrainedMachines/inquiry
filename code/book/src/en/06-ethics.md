# Ethics — Who Is Responsible for What a Machine Does?

AI generates code, makes recommendations, writes content. When it fails, who is at fault? This chapter convokes Aristotle, Kant, Mill, and Levinas to examine the major ethical frameworks and why choosing one deliberately — rather than defaulting unconsciously — is itself a skill.

The emergent tool: **ethical framework awareness.** Recognizing which ethical lens you're implicitly using when you deploy AI, and choosing with intention.
