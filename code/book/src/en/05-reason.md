# Reason — Deduction, Induction, Abduction, and the Machines

LLMs don't deduce or induce — they abduce. What does that mean for reliability? This chapter convokes Aristotle, Peirce, Popper, and Kahneman to examine the three modes of inference, why the machine's native mode is abduction, and what that means for how we must direct it.

This is the **pivot chapter** where the scientific method is established as the benchmark against which all other thinking tools in the book are measured.

The emergent tool: **structured reasoning.** Chain-of-thought as computational maieutics. Taxonomic thinking. Syllogistic prompt structure.
