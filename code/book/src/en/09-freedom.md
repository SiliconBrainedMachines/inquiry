# Freedom — Agency Without Will?

Autonomous agents. We say they "decide," but do they have agency? This chapter convokes the Stoics, Spinoza, Sartre, and the compatibilists to examine free will, determinism, and the practical question of what you control versus what the machine controls.

The emergent tool: **scope of control.** Understanding what the human directs (specifications, constraints, approval) and what the machine executes (generation within constraints). Applied Stoicism for the age of AI agents.
