# Truth — Can a Machine Lie?

Hallucinations. The LLM produces confident falsehoods. What is truth? This chapter convokes Parmenides, Heraclitus, Aquinas, Tarski, and Frankfurt to examine the nature of truth — and proposes that LLM hallucination is a third category beyond lying and bullshitting: generation without any relationship to truth at all.

The emergent tool: **fallacy detection.** The ancient taxonomy of bad arguments — argument from authority, false dichotomy, circular reasoning — as a modern defense against AI-generated plausible nonsense.
