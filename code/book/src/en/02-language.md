# Language — Can a Machine Mean?

Prompt engineering assumes language can precisely capture intent. But can it? This chapter convokes Plato, Zhuangzi, Wittgenstein, Saussure, and Derrida to examine what meaning is, how it works, and why word choice in prompts matters — not as a tip, but as a consequence of understanding how language itself operates.

The emergent tool: **semantic precision.** Not "be specific" — but understanding *why* specificity works, through the lens of 2,500 years of philosophy of language.
