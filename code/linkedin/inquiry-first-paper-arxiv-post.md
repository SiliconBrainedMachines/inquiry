# LinkedIn Post Draft — Inquiry First Paper

Inquiry now has its first paper.

The paper studies a question that matters for agentic software development: if you put
explicit inference-governing rules around the same underlying model, do you actually get
better engineering behavior, or just a richer record of what happened?

Our result is intentionally not triumphalist. The pilot does **not** show a simple
harness win. What it does show is more interesting:

- explicit harnessing reliably preserves a scoreable decision trail,
- apparent gains in evidence discipline and reconstructability are sensitive to how well
  the freestyle baseline is captured,
- overhead is the most stable result and remains substantial.

So the contribution is not only a tool. It is also a more honest way to study
transparency, traceability, and governance in agentic coding workflows.
