# Thesis Narrowing Decision

> **Type:** thesis
> **Status:** stable
> **Depends on:** [thesis.md](thesis.md), [results-section-draft.md](results-section-draft.md), [limitations-and-threats-to-validity.md](limitations-and-threats-to-validity.md), [evidence/pilot-claim-summary-t1-t2-t3.md](evidence/pilot-claim-summary-t1-t2-t3.md)
> **Used by:** [first-paper-checklist.md](first-paper-checklist.md), future manuscript draft, future discussion section

This document records the explicit post-pilot decision about how the first paper should
frame its thesis. The pilot does not support keeping the full strong-path framing as the
paper's operative results claim.

## Decision

Narrow the paper's framing from a pilot of the full strong-path thesis to a pilot that
supports a weaker methodological and practical claim.

The original thesis remains the motivating target of the research program. It does not
remain the most defensible description of what the completed pilot has actually shown.

## Why the thesis must narrow

Three pilot findings control this decision.

### C1 did not move in the predicted direction

Across T1, T2, and T3, the pilot does not show an observed harness advantage on
premature clarification. That leaves the maieutic arm of the strong path unconfirmed on
this task set.

### C2 and C3 do not show a stable Harness lead once capture is durable

T1 favors H on evidence-discipline and reconstructability under a conservative reread,
but T2 and T3 return those constructs to parity once the revised protocol preserves a
durable freestyle record. That means the current pilot cannot cleanly attribute the
early H advantage to the named operators themselves rather than to asymmetric capture
quality.

### C4 is the strongest stable result

The most durable cross-task result is not behavioral superiority on C1-C3. It is high
harness overhead. The paper must therefore treat cost as a first-class result rather
than as a side condition that can be subordinated to the original thesis framing.

## Narrowed claim for the first paper

The first paper should now be framed around the following narrower claim:

> Inquiry's harness reliably externalizes process and preserves a scoreable decision
> trail, but the apparent advantage of the harness on evidence-discipline and
> reconstructability is method-sensitive and can shrink toward parity once freestyle
> capture is made durably comparable; meanwhile, the harness imposes substantial and
> stable overhead that must be justified by task class rather than assumed away.

This narrower claim remains non-trivial. It still says something important about method,
measurement, and the practical cost profile of explicit inference governance. But it no
longer claims that the pilot has already shown a broad strong-path win for the named
operators.

## What remains live from the original thesis

The original thesis is not discarded. It is deferred to a stronger future test.

What remains live is the research possibility that named philosophical operators matter
operationally in ways generic scaffolding does not. What the pilot has shown so far is
that this stronger claim cannot be inferred cleanly from the current evidence without
overclaiming.

## Consequence for manuscript framing

The paper should now do four things explicitly:

1. present the pilot as a methodological correction against overclaiming,
2. report C4 overhead as the most stable positive result,
3. describe C2 and C3 as mixed and strongly affected by capture discipline,
4. state that the strong-path claim remains unconfirmed rather than weakly supported.

## Consequence for future studies

If a later study wants to revive the strong path, it will need a design that isolates
operator effect from generic structure and from capture-quality asymmetry more cleanly
than the current pilot does. Until then, the first paper should prefer the narrower
framing above.
