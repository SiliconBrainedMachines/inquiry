# Short Paper Surrogate Template

> **Type:** plan
> **Status:** stable
> **Depends on:** [submission-target-and-formatting-decision.md](submission-target-and-formatting-decision.md), [first-paper-shell.md](first-paper-shell.md)
> **Used by:** [manuscript-assembly-draft.md](manuscript-assembly-draft.md), [first-paper-checklist.md](first-paper-checklist.md), future venue-specific manuscript draft

This document fixes a concrete internal surrogate for the first paper before any real
CFP or publisher template is chosen. It is intentionally ACM-like, but it is not tied
to one publisher's exact macros. Its job is to force the manuscript into a believable
short-paper shape now, so later venue adaptation becomes a constrained rewrite rather
than a structural surprise.

## Surrogate target

Treat the current paper as a two-column short paper with a main-text budget equivalent
to about 7-8 pages plus references.

The surrogate assumes one title block, one abstract, one keyword line, five numbered
main sections, and no appendix-dependent argumentation.

## Required front matter

The manuscript should expose these fields explicitly.

1. Title.
2. Abstract.
3. Keywords.

No author block, acknowledgments, artifact badge language, or appendix note is required
at this stage.

## Section order

The surrogate manuscript should use this order.

1. Introduction.
2. Theoretical Framing.
3. Inquiry and Pilot Method.
4. Results.
5. Discussion and Limits.

This order is now the concrete substitute for a real venue template unless a later CFP
forces a materially different arrangement.

## Space discipline

Use these internal targets while drafting.

- Abstract: 180-220 words.
- Introduction: about 0.9-1.2 pages.
- Theoretical Framing: about 0.6-0.9 pages.
- Inquiry and Pilot Method: about 1.2-1.6 pages.
- Results: about 1.6-2.2 pages, including one main table.
- Discussion and Limits: about 0.9-1.2 pages.

These are not publishing rules. They are pressure constraints to keep the paper honest
about what a short contribution can carry.

## Display-item rule

The main body gets one controlling cross-task table by default. Do not assume room for
multiple per-task tables or figures unless a later concrete venue clearly permits them.

## What must stay in the main body

Even under space pressure, do not push these into notes or appendix-only support.

- the narrowed claim,
- the capture-sensitivity result,
- the overhead result,
- the central threats to validity.

If space must be recovered, cut repeated philosophical exposition, per-task detail that
is already subsumed by the cross-task table, and generic future-work prose before
cutting those items.

## What this surrogate forbids

Do not let the manuscript drift into any of the following shapes.

- a full empirical victory paper,
- a benchmark-heavy model-capability paper,
- a philosophy-first exposition where the artifact and method become secondary.

Those shapes would reintroduce claims the current pilot does not authorize.
