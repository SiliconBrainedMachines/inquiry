This document is the assembly shell for the first paper. It is not the full manuscript.
Its job is to define the section order, the controlling source documents for each
section, and the working argumentative flow now that the pilot has forced a narrower
thesis framing. It now also assumes the short workshop-style publication shape fixed in
[submission-target-and-formatting-decision.md](submission-target-and-formatting-decision.md).

## Working title direction

Inquiry as Engineering of Inference: A Pilot Comparison of Explicit Harness Rules and
Freestyle LLM-Assisted Software Work

This is a direction, not a frozen title. The crucial constraint is that the title should
not promise a broad strong-path win that the current pilot does not support.

## Section order

### 1. Problem

**Purpose:** state the engineering problem as one of inference governance rather than raw
model capability.

**Primary sources:** [research-line.md](research-line.md), [concepts/harness-vs-capability.md](concepts/harness-vs-capability.md)

**Working move:** frame LLM-assisted software work as powerful but under-governed
abduction, then motivate why explicit rules over asking, doubting, acting, and leaving
a trail are an engineering surface.

### 2. Contribution

**Purpose:** state the paper's non-trivial claim without collapsing into generic
"philosophy helps" rhetoric.

**Primary sources:** [contribution.md](contribution.md), [thesis-narrowing-decision.md](thesis-narrowing-decision.md)

**Working move:** say that philosophical lineage is being treated as a source of
operator taxonomy for engineering, while making clear that the pilot now supports this
more strongly as a methodological program than as a completed strong-path empirical win.

### 3. Theoretical framing

**Purpose:** define the named operator lineage and why explicit harness rules are the
delivery mechanism.

**Primary sources:** [thesis.md](thesis.md), [concepts/abductive-machine.md](concepts/abductive-machine.md), [concepts/thinking-tools.md](concepts/thinking-tools.md), [operators/maieutics.md](operators/maieutics.md), [operators/methodic-doubt.md](operators/methodic-doubt.md), [operators/abductive-inquiry.md](operators/abductive-inquiry.md)

**Working move:** present the original strong-path target as the motivating theory, then
signal that the pilot will test it under conditions where it is allowed to fail.

### 4. Artifact

**Purpose:** describe Inquiry as the concrete harness under study.

**Primary sources:** [docs/architecture.md](../../docs/architecture.md), [experimental-protocol.md](experimental-protocol.md)

**Working move:** describe the harness as FSM structure plus named operators, durable
artifacts, and trace-producing workflow, contrasted with host-native freestyle use of
the same model.

### 5. Method

**Purpose:** present the frozen comparative pilot design, task corpus, measures, and
scoring procedure.

**Primary sources:** [method-section-draft.md](method-section-draft.md)

**Working move:** carry this section with minimal rewriting from the existing draft.

### 6. Results

**Purpose:** report T1-T3 claim by claim using the controlling second-pass readings.

**Primary sources:** [results-section-draft.md](results-section-draft.md)

**Working move:** foreground overhead as the most stable positive result, keep C1 null,
and treat C2/C3 as mixed and capture-sensitive.

### 7. Limits

**Purpose:** surface threats to validity before discussion prose widens the claim.

**Primary sources:** [limitations-and-threats-to-validity.md](limitations-and-threats-to-validity.md)

**Working move:** make explicit that the pilot does not yet isolate operator effect from
generic structure and capture asymmetry, and that the current evidence is single-repo,
Windows-coupled, and task-bounded.

### 8. Discussion

**Purpose:** interpret the pilot as a methodological correction and a practical cost
finding rather than as broad confirmation of the original thesis.

**Primary sources:** [thesis-narrowing-decision.md](thesis-narrowing-decision.md), [results-section-draft.md](results-section-draft.md), [limitations-and-threats-to-validity.md](limitations-and-threats-to-validity.md)

**Working move:** argue that the pilot's strongest lesson is about protocol design and
the cost of explicit governance, while deferring the full strong-path thesis to a later,
cleaner study.

## Assembly rule

When the manuscript is drafted, sections should be composed from the source documents
above rather than rewritten from scratch. This is the main protection against narrative
drift now that the pilot has forced a narrower framing.
