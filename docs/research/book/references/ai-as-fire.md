## The Argument (for book use)

The analogy is not hyperbolic. Each major inflection in human history externalized a biological capability:

| Technology | Externalized | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | Energy (digestion, warmth, metallurgy) | ~1M years ago |
| Writing | Memory | ~5,000 years ago |
| Printing press | Distribution of knowledge | ~1450 CE |
| Computers | Calculation | ~1945 CE |
| LLMs | Language processing and reasoning | ~2022 CE |

LLMs are the first technology to externalize **language comprehension and production** — something that until 2022 was exclusively biological. The prologue's claim is that this is at least as significant as fire, because fire changed what we could *do* while language-capable AI changes how we can *think*.

## Marketing Angle

The phrase "the largest event for humanity since the domestication of fire" — used independently by the book's author and echoed by the CEO of the world's largest AI company — can serve as a tagline or back-cover pull quote, with Pichai's parallel as supporting context.
