# Title Direction

**Status:** Active title decision
**Last updated:** 2026-05-17

---

## Current Decision

The book's new working title is:

**Critique of Pure Abduction**

This replaces the provisional title **Philo SophIA** as the primary title candidate.

---

## Why This Title Wins

### 1. It contains the thesis

The title already states the book's central philosophical and technical claim: large language models are abductive machines, and what is needed is not better rhetoric about their outputs, but a critique of the legitimacy, scope, and limits of their inferential leaps.

### 2. It uses "critique" in the Kantian sense

Here, **critique** does not mean a negative review. It means an examination of conditions, authority, limits, and legitimate use. The model is not merely being criticized; its abductive power is being placed before a tribunal.

### 3. It is more memorable than Philo SophIA

**Philo SophIA** is broad and brand-like. **Critique of Pure Abduction** is specific, arresting, and programmatic. It sounds like a real intellectual project rather than a general philosophy-of-AI label.

### 4. It aligns with the book's actual arc

The prologue already moves toward this structure:

- the machine speaks fluently
- the machine confabulates structurally
- the failure is abductive, not merely accidental
- philosophy provides the tools for governing that failure

This title names that arc directly.

---

## Subtitle

Previous proposed subtitle:

**The Thinking Tools Humanity Built for the Age of Language Machines**

Current judgment: **no subtitle is required**.

Reason: the new main title already carries enough conceptual weight and specificity. The subtitle was more necessary when the main title was **Philo SophIA**, which was broader and less self-explanatory.

The subtitle can still be retained as optional marketing or cover copy, but it is no longer necessary as part of the canonical title.

---

## Naming Notes

- **English canonical title:** Critique of Pure Abduction
- **Kantian resonance:** intentional and desirable
- **Possible German echo for internal or thematic use:** Kritik der reinen Abduktion
- **Canonical public title recommendation:** use the English title alone

---

## Consequences

This title shift affects:

- positioning of the book as a philosophical intervention, not just a general introduction
- the framing of the prologue and eventual introduction
- the naming direction for the Inquiry skill, where **Kritik** remains a strong candidate because it preserves the Kantian sense of critique more explicitly than ordinary English usage

---

## Current Recommendation

Use:

**Critique of Pure Abduction**

Do not require a subtitle unless a later publishing or marketing reason makes one useful.
