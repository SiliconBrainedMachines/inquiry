## Summary

The Analyze phase established that AI is applied philosophy by direct genealogy, and that the philosophical tools humanity built over 2,500 years are the correct tools to direct AI — not by accident, but by inheritance. The book addresses two thinking systems — the human mind and the abductive mind — and the communication gap between them. Its practical goal is cognitive transparency (*perspicuitas*): reducing the distance between intention and expression to zero. The book's structure, thesis, audience, competitive position, risks, design decisions, and supporting models are documented above. The next phase (Plan) will research sources, finalize philosopher selection, outline argument arcs per chapter, and produce a sample chapter as proof of concept.
