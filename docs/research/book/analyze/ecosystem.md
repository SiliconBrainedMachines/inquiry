# Philo SophIA — The Ecosystem

**Source:** Analysis sessions, March 2026

---

## Three Projects, One Vision

Philo SophIA exists within a three-project ecosystem where each project feeds the others:

| Project | Role | Status |
|---------|------|--------|
| **Philo SophIA** | Book — intellectual foundation, diagnosis, knowledge | In analysis (Analyze complete) |
| **APE** | Framework — the tool that builds everything else | In development (Analyze complete) |
| **Trivium** | App — trains cognitive skills at scale, AI as tutor | Concept stage |

## Relationships

- **The book** diagnoses the problem and presents the knowledge.
- **The app** operationalizes the book's tools as trainable exercises.
- **APE** is the living case study where these tools are applied to software, and the methodology used to build everything.

Each feeds the others: the book provides the intellectual foundation for APE and Trivium; APE provides the development methodology; Trivium demonstrates that the book's thinking tools can be practiced, not just understood.

## Execution Sequence

**Philo SophIA first** (provides intellectual foundation) → APE development informed by the book → Trivium development using APE → all three publish together.

**Priority:** book > APE > app.

**Rationale for priority change:** Writing the book is the theoretical exercise needed before building the framework. The book forces rigorous articulation of the thesis; APE benefits from that clarity.

## Trivium — The App Concept

**Name:** Trivium
**Tagline:** "The classical education strikes back" (working — to be refined)
**Alternatives explored and parked:** Organon, LogIA, Paideia, Nous, TechnIA. "Trivium Strikes Back" considered as tagline/marketing, not as app name.

### What It Is

A cognitive gymnasium. Not "learn philosophy" but "train thinking skills rooted in 2,500 years of philosophy." Duolingo/Brilliant model with AI as adaptive tutor.

### Hook

"Learn to use AI better than 99% of people — not with prompting tricks, but with thinking tools that are 2,500 years old."

### Exercise Types (Verifiable)

- **Fallacy detection** — taxonomy-based, drawn from the book's failure taxonomy
- **Argument construction** — formal validity, syllogistic structure
- **Socratic questioning** — evaluated by quality of questions generated
- **Epistemic classification** — fact vs. inference vs. opinion vs. hallucination
- **Semantic precision** — ambiguity reduction with measurable AI output improvement

### Connection to the Book

Each chapter's emergent thinking tool maps to a trainable, testable exercise in the app. The book teaches *why*; the app trains *how*.

### The New Trivium (Research Required)

Education must return to foundations of thought. Candidate composition for the AI era:

1. **Semantics** — how language creates meaning
2. **Epistemology** — what kinds of reasoning to apply and when
3. **Ethics** — what deserves to be communicated and why

Final composition to be determined through research into the historical trivium/quadrivium, modern cognitive science, and AI-specific requirements.
