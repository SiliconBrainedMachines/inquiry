# Philo SophIA — Risks and Mitigations

**Source:** Analysis sessions + competitive landscape research, March 2026

---

## Book Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Superficial treatment (too many philosophers, too little depth) | Brutal selection: only philosophers who uniquely illuminate AI. Quality over completeness. |
| Non-Western traditions as tokenism | Same rigor standard as Western. Include only if unique contribution to AI understanding. |
| Co-creation perception ("the AI wrote it") | Human voice (engineer-philosopher with genuine astonishment) must be protected. Epilogue handles transparency. |
| Two-books-in-one tension (philosophy vs. practical tools) | Tools emerge from understanding, never as separate sections. One book, layered reading. |
| Subtitle dating (GPT may become irrelevant) | "From Thales to GPT" is working tagline. Final version chosen closer to publication. |
| Market positioning ("philosophy of AI" sounds academic) | Subtitle must communicate accessibility. Cover design and blurb must signal general audience. |

## Positioning Risks (from Competitive Landscape)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Confused with "AI ethics" shelf (saturated: Christian, Crawford, Vallor, Floridi, etc.) | Ethics is one chapter of ten, not the book. Subtitle and structure make scope clear. |
| LLM-specific primers eat the language/truth space (Poibeau 2025, Coeckelbergh/Gunkel 2025) | GPT is a case study within a 2500-year arc, not the subject. |
| Timing — more competitors by publication date | The panoramic + genealogy + practical tools combination is too ambitious for fast-publish competitors. |

## Selection Criteria for Philosophers

A philosopher is included if and only if they **illuminate something specific about AI that no one else illuminates.** This applies equally to Western and non-Western traditions. No tokenism — if a tradition earns its place through unique insight, it's included with full rigor. If it doesn't, it's not.

## Market Context

The "AI guru" market is saturated with superficial courses and quick-tip content. Philo SophIA must be a solid, real proposal — not another "10 prompts that will change your life." The differentiation is depth and genealogy: showing that the tools work *because* they derive from the same intellectual tradition that created AI itself.
